Competitor ranking & name they own

Ordered by relevance to the high-Na / serious-sweat powder beachhead in India. One-liner = the mental territory each rival occupies.

1

UP&RUN

NAME THEY OWN — Action-compound

High-sodium, zero-sugar powder sachets for Indian heat. Advance at 1000 mg Na / 0 g sugar / ~₹40 per serve; kids Salties; Equinox lab + WADA/NADA; pediatrician-study claim; founder Chanakya Shah’s Bandra run club. Thycure Pharma · founded 2024, Mumbai.

1000 mg Na 0 g sugar ~₹40/serve
2

Flow Hydration

NAME THEY OWN — Event hydration

API lists 1000 mg Na, 200 mg K, 60 mg Mg, 0 g sugar at ~₹40–50/serve. Homepage still markets 12.5 g / 6.9 g glucose ORS-style story — internally inconsistent. Events / marathons focus.

1000 mg Na (API) ₹40–50/serve Messaging chaos
3

Unived Elite

NAME THEY OWN — Race-grade

Race-grade 1000 mg Na, betaine + bicarbonate, endurance community trust; tub format; functional sucrose (~62 kcal). Owns serious runners/triathletes who want cotransport science.

1000 mg Na 62 kcal ₹37.5–75/serve
4

Fast&Up Reload

NAME THEY OWN — Gym shout

Dominant effervescent franchise (~180 mg Na, Informed Choice, ~₹10–12/serve) plus Reload Salts+ (~900 mg Na, 0 sugar). Owns gym/Amazon shelf and “drop-fizz-drink” habit. Aeronutrix.

Reload 180 Na Salts+ ~900 Informed Choice
5

Liquid I.V. (HUL)

NAME THEY OWN — Stick culture

Mass awareness, modern trade + D2C. India PDP does not clearly publish Na mg; global panels ~500 mg Na + ~11 g sugar. Premium ₹/serve. Sugar-free Na on India PDP still UNKNOWN.

~₹96/stick HUL reach India Na UNKNOWN
6

MuscleBlaze Hydr8

NAME THEY OWN — Gym shout

Effervescent ~222 mg Na, BCAAs, no-added-sugar, ~₹10–11/serve. HealthKart ecosystem price floor. Not a high-Na powder peer.

222 mg Na ~₹10–11/serve BCAAs
7

Gatorade

NAME THEY OWN — Sports myth

RTD sugar sports drink. Typical ~225–250 mg Na / ~30 g sugar per 500 ml; ~₹50/bottle. Impulse “sports drink” occasion — not powder peer.

~₹50/bottle High sugar Occasion stealer
7

Electral ORS

NAME THEY OWN — Clinic ORS

Medical WHO-style ORS. ~₹4.6 per 4.4 g sachet; ~340 mg Na (calc). Chemist mental model for “I’m drained” — illness / acute dehydration frame.

~₹4.6 ~340 mg Na Chemist default
